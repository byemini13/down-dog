## Design Context

### Users
Personal use on a phone placed near the mat, following timed stretches and spoken instructions. The user needs demonstrations that make body position easy to understand at a glance.

### Brand Personality
Calm, clear, simple. Preserve the existing warm palette and restrained typography.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user confirmed clear studio-style images of a person. Use uncluttered backgrounds, visible limbs, and full-body framing. Keep the interface focused on the stretch, side, and remaining time.

### Design Principles
- Make the demonstrated position legible before adding decoration.
- Show the whole body without cropping hands or feet.
- Keep spoken and visual instructions consistent.
- Use large controls that are easy to tap from the mat.
- Preserve the calm, simple character of the existing app.
